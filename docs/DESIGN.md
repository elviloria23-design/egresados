## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital identity for the Alumni and Guajiro Talent Portal, anchored in the hospitality philosophy of Riohacha. The brand personality balances warm, welcoming hospitality with professional talent elevation. It bridges the cultural vibrancy of La Guajira—its desert sun, sea breeze, and artisanal geometric motifs—with contemporary corporate credibility.

The visual style is **Contemporary Coastal Modernism**:
- Sun-bleached warm sand backdrops evoke comfort and relaxed coastal elegance.
- Crisp charcoal typography provides institutional weight and high legibility.
- Vivid chromatic accents drawn from the corporate emblem—sunset terracotta, golden ochre, and Caribbean teal—are used strategically to highlight professional achievements, alumni success stories, and dynamic callouts.
- Geometry balances structured grids with soft, hospitable contours, reflecting genuine service delivered with passion.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the corporate iconography while ensuring AA/AAA accessibility compliance across all digital surfaces:

- **Primary (`#F15A24` Guajira Orange)**: Carries the signature passion and warmth of the brand. Reserved for primary actions, highlight badges, and focal interactions.
- **Secondary (`#0E9AA7` Caribbean Teal)**: Reflects the Caribbean coastline of Riohacha. Used for talent categories, skill tags, verified alumni badges, and complementary links.
- **Tertiary (`#E4B726` Sun Ochre)**: Conveys warmth, optimism, and celebration. Employed for ratings, awards, spotlight banners, and delicate decorative indicators.
- **Neutral (`#2B2C2D` Charcoal)**: Grounding charcoal foundation used for high-contrast typography, crisp borders, and dark thematic containers.

Backgrounds intentionally depart from harsh pure white in favor of `sand-canvas` (`#FDFCF7`) and `sand-surface` (`#F7F5EC`), creating a hospitable, tactile environment reminiscent of coastal sands.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Outfit** for structural headers with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interface mechanics:

- **Outfit** carries geometric clarity with rounded, humanistic proportions. It directly mirrors the clean, rounded brand glyphs while asserting editorial refinement in talent presentations.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers optimal legibility across talent bios, resume overviews, credential tables, and navigation elements.

Uppercase styling is applied to micro-labels and eyebrow tags with gentle letter-spacing (`0.04em`), matching the spaced aesthetic (`a t e n d e m o s`) seen in the brand identity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid system anchored by relaxed horizontal margins that preserve breathing room:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and a max canvas width of `1280px` centered within generous 3rem (`48px`) margins. Section padding uses `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to reinforce an unhurried, hospitality-focused rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) screen margins.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to multiples of `0.25rem` (`4px`), pairing compact gap spacing (`space-sm`) inside compound UI groups with expansive gaps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) between distinct narrative sections.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or heavy drop shadows, instead utilizing warm, ambient depth that mirrors bright Caribbean daylight:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Baseline canvas (`#FDFCF7`) with clean surface separation created by subtle borders (`1px solid #E5E1D3`).
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting)**: Surface containers use pure `#FFFFFF` over `#FDFCF7`, elevated with a warm diffuse shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 44, 45, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(241, 90, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating)**: Interactive cards and talent profiles lift on hover: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 44, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(241, 90, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Overlays)**: Dialogs and floating recruitment filters use: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(43, 44, 45, 0.16)`.
- **Accent Glow**: Select interactive CTA buttons adopt a soft tinted under-glow matching their background hue (e.g., `0 6px 16px rgba(241, 90, 36, 0.28)` for primary orange actions).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Level 2 (Rounded)** approach with selected full-pill elements, reflecting the curved stems of the corporate monogram:

- **Standard Components**: Buttons, text inputs, card wrappers, and media frames use `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Feature Cards & Talent Spotlights**: Large cards employ `1rem` (`16px`) corner radiuses for an approachable frame.
- **Pill Accents**: Action chips, category tags, search bars, and floating badges use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to echo the pill-shaped booking button from the flagship hotel UI.
- **Geometric Accents**: Subtle Wayuu-inspired triangular accent motifs and angled color-corner trims can be subtly introduced on media card edges to celebrate Guajira culture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Guajira Orange (`#F15A24`) with pure white text, semi-bold typography, and subtle pill or `rounded-lg` silhouette. Hover states transition smoothly to Desert Amber (`#E97835`) with an ambient warm glow.
- **Secondary**: Crisp Charcoal outline (`1.5px solid #2B2C2D`) or Caribbean Teal outline (`1.5px solid #0E9AA7`) with matching text color, turning filled on hover.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background with charcoal text and an underlined animated hover state.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding.
- **Specialty/Department Tag**: Warm tinted background (Teal at 10% opacity `#0E9AA71A`) with Teal `#0E9AA7` text.
- **Alumni Status Tag**: Ochre at 12% opacity (`#E4B7261F`) with deep ochre/charcoal text.

### Cards (Talent & Alumni Showcase)
- Built on pure white `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by an ultra-fine border (`#E5E1D3`).
- Header includes talent avatar, verified alumni badge, and specialty chip.
- Content area features concise biography, current placement/hospitality role, and key skills.
- Footer features a direct contact or portfolio CTA with hover-card lift (Level 2 elevation).

### Input Fields & Search
- Inputs sit on `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px solid #E5E1D3` perimeter, transitioning to a primary orange focus ring (`2px solid #F15A24`) on active states.
- Global directory search utilizes a pill-shaped layout with integrated category dropdown and teal search trigger.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular radio buttons and soft-square checkboxes (`rounded-sm`). Checked states fill with `#F15A24` displaying a crisp white icon.

### Hospitality Accents & Testimonial Banners
- Testimonial containers incorporate soft sand-surface shading (`#F7F5EC`), charcoal italic quotes, and an authentic warm signature motif recalling the phrase *"atendemos con pasión"*.
